# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We aim to provide a respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in communication
- Assume good intent
- Give technical feedback with clear rationale
- Focus on issues, not people

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination
- Publishing private/personal data without consent
- Repeated disruptive behavior in issues/PRs

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block contributors that violate this code.
